🚀 What I'm Building

🐄 HerdSense AI

The intelligence layer for livestock.

HerdSense AI is an AI + IoT livestock intelligence platform designed to connect livestock, sensors, telemetry, analytics, and farmer decision-making into one intelligent layer.

The platform focuses on transforming livestock and environmental data into actionable intelligence around:

- Animal health signals
- Temperature
- Location
- Movement and activity
- Feeding and intake-related signals
- Environmental conditions
- Telemetry
- Anomalies
- Potential health risks
- Historical livestock data

The long-term vision is to build infrastructure that can support digital livestock identity, traceability, livestock financing, insurance infrastructure, and connected livestock systems.

🔗 Website: https://herdsense-ai.lovable.app/
